# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- Focused Windows portable workspace export/import with local key initialization and recovery-key export.
- Direct guarded acquisition, encrypted signed packages, independent verification and safe filesystem reconstruction.
- Empty-directory inventories, stricter history/mapping/ZIP64 admission and packaged offline acceptance.
- Bundled pinned MinGit and age-keygen; no developer checkout or recovery-time downloads.
- Removed native activation and superseded VM/offline-authority/attestation certification machinery.

Earlier candidate evidence remains privately preserved and is not certification of this release.
