# Support boundary

Supported content and refusals are defined in [compatibility](docs/compatibility.md). Report issues with synthetic reproductions, version, safe error code and exit status. Do not attach business archives, keys or raw conversation diagnostics.

This product creates portable filesystem recovery. It does not restore native account/cloud history or provide an SLA. Company approval must establish a monitored support/security route before public distribution.

This is an engineering showcase, not a managed backup or supported recovery service. Publication creates no maintenance, support, or recovery obligation. Users remain responsible for their use and independent backups; see [LICENSE](LICENSE) for warranty and liability terms.
